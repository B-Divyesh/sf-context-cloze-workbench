Use

The tool is provided as-is. Keep a JSON backup if your sentence bank matters to you; browser storage can be cleared by the device owner or browser.

Content

The app includes no sentence corpus and does not publish your prompts. Do not rely on answer matching as a substitute for a teacher’s judgment.
